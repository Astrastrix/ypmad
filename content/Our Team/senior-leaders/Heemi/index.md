Rosedale - Graduated as a Leader in 2022

I love MAD because it genuinely brings out the best version of me. Its a me that feels both safe and loved but most importantly gives me the strength to know that everything will be okay. Honestly at first I chose to be leader so I could just see the same people that made me feel so loved but over time I realised how much good I was bringing to the camp and fully committed on being the best leader that suited me. The best thing this camp gave me was that it made me a better person for others but most importantly for myself.
